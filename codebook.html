ThemeLook forTestOptional label
NNeedWhat must be in place? “I have to…”, “I can’t… without…”, “there’s nowhere to…”, “otherwise I fall behind” If it were missing, would they be blocked, or just annoyed? Annoyed means preference. None
GGoalWhat do they want to achieve? “I want to…”, “I’m trying to…”, “so that I can…”, “I’d like to be able to…” Can you finish “They want to achieve…”? Practical · Social · Emotional
MMotivationWhy do they do it? “because…”, “that’s why I…”, “I get stressed when…”, “I’m afraid of missing out” Does the quote answer “why?” Intrinsic (inside) · Extrinsic (outside)
BBehaviourWhat do they actually do? “I usually…”, “last week I…”, “first I…, then I…”, “I always…” Could you film it? Habit · Pain point · Workaround
CContextWhere, who with, when? “in the canteen”, “with friends”, “between classes”, “during exam week” Is it the setting, not the action or the wish? Physical · Social · Temporal

Surprised? Star it

If a quote surprises you or fits none of the five themes, add a star. Do not force it into a theme. Starred quotes matter most in class.

Two codes in one quote

Highlight each part in its own colour. “45 minutes between classes” is context. “I need food that’s fast” is a need.

A wish is not behaviour

“I’d like to go three times a week” is a goal. “Last week I went once” is behaviour. A general claim needs a specific example to check it.

Bring to class: your coded transcripts, starred quotes marked · English versions of your sticky-note quotes if you interviewed in Dutch · a reference for every quote (participant and timestamp, for example P3, 04:32).
